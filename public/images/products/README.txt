Drop product photos here, then reference them in data/products.ts.

Naming convention (recommended): use the product slug.
  e.g. sewer-jetting-machine.jpg  ->  images: ["/images/products/sewer-jetting-machine.jpg"]

Multiple images per product are supported (first = main):
  images: [
    "/images/products/sewer-jetting-machine.jpg",
    "/images/products/sewer-jetting-machine-2.jpg",
  ]

Recommended: 4:3 ratio, ~1200x900px, JPG/WebP, under ~300KB each.
Until an image is added, a branded "image coming soon" placeholder is shown.
